## Why two

The book makes its argument by running code, so nearly every page holds a working
program. Two licenses fall out of that one fact.

A NonCommercial term on the prose leaves a print edition worth publishing, which
is the arrangement every free R book of this kind uses. A permissive license on
the code means a reader can copy an example straight into a real project, which
is the only thing the examples are for. A single license could not do both.
